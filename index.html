Step 4 — Format & Approach

Choose your format.

Select how your output will be structured and paced.

Starting from an idea produces a short film. Feature and series lengths are built from a full manuscript — there simply isn't enough source material in a story outline to write 95 honest pages, and padding it would show. Upload a manuscript to unlock the longer formats.

🎬 Feature Film

A standalone film for theatrical or streaming release.

Target: 90–110 pages · ~95 min

📺 Limited Series

Multi-episode series for complex, sprawling stories.

2–8 episodes · 40–55 min each

📡 TV Pilot

First episode that sets up the world and hooks viewers.

Target: 50–60 pages · ~50 min

🎞️ Short Film

Focused adaptation of a key scene or chapter.

Target: 10–30 pages · 10–30 min
Compress — Every subplot and character arc from your source is preserved, but scenes are tightened, dialogue is trimmed, and descriptions are cut to fit the target page count. Best when your story is already well-structured and you want nothing left out.
Select Best — The AI identifies the most cinematic, dramatic, and emotionally powerful scenes from your source and builds the screenplay around those. Weaker subplots may be dropped entirely. Best for sprawling books where ruthless editing is needed.
Faithful — Stays as close to the source material as possible — same scene order, same dialogue where practical, same pacing. Ideal when you have an existing adaptation contract or need to stay true to the book for rights reasons.

Sweet spot by genre (for traditional publishing)
Middle-grade: 20–55K words
YA: 55–80K (sweet spot ~70K)
Romance: 50–90K (category romance often 50–75K)
Mystery / Thriller: 70–90K — tight pacing rewarded
Literary fiction: 80–100K — above 110K needs to earn it
Sci-fi / Fantasy: 90–120K (epic fantasy up to 150K)
Historical fiction: 100–120K
Debut-author reality check. Most agents auto-reject debut novels above 100K words unless it's epic fantasy or historical. Below 50K is too short for the "novel" market (except middle-grade / novella). Aim for the sweet spot of your genre, not the ceiling.
Self-publishing? Word count → pages → retail price. Trade paperback rule of thumb is ~250 words/page, so a 60K-word book ≈ 240 pages.
Under 200 pages (~50K words) — hard to price above $9.99, looks slim on a shelf
200–300 pages (~50–75K) — sweet spot: $12.99–14.99
300–400 pages (~75–100K) — $14.99–16.99
400+ pages (~100K+) — priced $16.99+ but per-page print cost on KDP starts eating royalties fast
